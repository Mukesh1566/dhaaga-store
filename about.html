Our story

A small range of menswear, made to be worn often.

Dhaaga began with a simple frustration: finding a good shirt in the right size shouldn't take a whole Saturday. We decided to make fewer things and make them well.

Our range covers what a man actually wears: tees and polos, Oxford and linen shirts, chinos and denim, kurtas for every occasion, and jackets for the cooler months. Every piece is cut with an Indian build in mind.

We choose breathable cotton, linen and denim, and we keep our fits consistent from one season to the next, so a size that works once keeps working.
